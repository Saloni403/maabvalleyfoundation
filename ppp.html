“Empowering Farmers, Youth & Communities through Sustainable Growth”

MAAB VALLEY FOUNDATION (MVF) is a movement dedicated to empowering rural Kashmir by transforming small and landless farmers into self-reliant entrepreneurs. The Foundation combines organic farming, agrotourism, and innovation to build sustainable livelihoods, promote eco-conscious living, and revive traditional agricultural pride. It stands for inclusive growth—where every farmer, youth, and woman becomes a key stakeholder in rural progress.

Key Components

Farmer Empowerment

Training, demonstration farms, and direct market linkages.

Youth Empowerment

Agro-startups, innovation, and modern farm technologies.

Women Empowerment

Cottage industries, packaging, and leadership roles.

Community Empowerment

Joint ventures, rooftop farming, and AMC service models.

“Choose Organic – Empower Rural Kashmir

To build a self-sustaining and empowered rural Kashmir where every farmer becomes an entrepreneur, every youth a change-maker, and every village a model of green growth and opportunity.

Expected Outcomes:
  • => Uplift the rural economy through organic farming, value addition, and farm-based enterprises.
  • => Create livelihood opportunities through agrotourism, training, and joint ventures.
  • => Empower women and youth with access to skills, innovation, and entrepreneurship support.
  • => Preserve Kashmir’s natural heritage through eco-conscious and sustainable agricultural systems.
